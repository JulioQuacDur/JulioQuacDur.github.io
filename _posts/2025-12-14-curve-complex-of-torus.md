If you are familiar with the [fundamental group][wiki_fundamental_group]
of a space, you already heard before about the idea
using an algebraic structures to _'parametrize'_ or _'describe'_
topological information of a space. Borrowing the same idea, 
in this post we will be interested on roughly describing what I call the
_'cutting'_ curves of a surface by using the combinatorial object known 
as __The Curve Complex__.

## Types surfaces.

We will be dealing with closed surfaces of a given genus $$g$$ with
$$p$$ punctures, denoted by $$S_{g,p}$$. If we delete $$n$$ disk from
the surface $$S_{g,p}$$ we say it has $$n$$ holes and denote it by
$$S_{g,p,n}$$ in this case, we notice that the bundary of the surface
is made of $$n$$ holes.
